# Zynygram Social Network Overview

## Official information
- Zynygram is an Uzbek social network designed for social interaction, communication, and modern creative workflows.
- Users can publish posts (matnlar, rasmlar, videolar) and communicate directly with other users on the platform.
- Zynygram includes advanced AI-powered functionality that allows users to generate images and videos directly within the platform.
- Platform support and AI assistant operate in Uzbek, Russian, and English.

## Procedures
- Publishing posts: Users can create and share posts within their user profile and feed.
- Communication: Users can send direct messages and interact with publications from other users.
- AI Generation: Users can access AI tools within the Zynygram interface to generate images and videos based on textual prompts.
