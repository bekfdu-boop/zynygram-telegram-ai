# Advertising and Partnerships (Reklama va Hamkorlik)

## Official information
- Zynygram offers opportunities for brands, businesses, and creators to reach the audience across the social network.
- All commercial campaigns and advertising activities must comply with platform content guidelines and regional legislation.
- OFFICIAL POLICY REQUIRED: Official ad formats, rate cards, self-serve advertising dashboard, and commercial partnership terms.

## Procedures
- Partnership inquiries:
  - Users interested in advertising collaborations or commercial sponsorship should be directed to the official advertising department.
  - OFFICIAL POLICY REQUIRED: Contact emails, sales team usernames, or automated advertising portal URLs.
  - Inquiries for custom advertising packages should be escalated to human operators using `/human`.

## Frequently asked questions
- **Zynygram-da reklama berish narxi qancha? (How much does advertising cost?)**
  Reklama narxlari va hamkorlik paketlari bo'yicha rasmiy tariflar hozircha tasdiqlanmoqda. Aniq taklif va ma'lumot olish uchun operatorimizga bog'lanishingiz mumkin (/human).
- **Qanday reklama turlari mavjud?**
  OFFICIAL POLICY REQUIRED. Rasmiy reklama formatlari (lentadagi postlar, bannerlar, homiylik loyihalari) bo'yicha ma'lumotlar rasmiy e'lon qilinadi.

## Notes
- Never invent pricing numbers, CPM rates, or payment guarantees.
- Always recommend `/human` for commercial proposals.
