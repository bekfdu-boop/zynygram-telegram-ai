# Payments and Billing (To'lovlar va Hisob-kitob)

## Official information
- Payment operations on Zynygram are intended for premium features, subscription tiers, or AI generation token purchases.
- All payments are processed securely through certified payment gateways.
- OFFICIAL POLICY REQUIRED: List of supported payment providers (e.g. Uzum Bank, Payme, Click, international cards), pricing plans, and refund policies.

## Procedures
- Payment issues:
  - If a user reports an erroneous charge, double charge, or uncredited purchase, escalate immediately to a human operator.
  - Never ask the user for credit card numbers, CVV/CVC codes, bank SMS verification codes, or card PINs.
  - Ask only for the transaction reference ID, date, and approximate time if necessary.
- OFFICIAL POLICY REQUIRED: Automated refund procedure and billing inquiry dashboard.

## Frequently asked questions
- **To'lov qanday usullarda qabul qilinadi? (What payment methods are supported?)**
  Hozirgi vaqtda to'lov tizimlari bo'yicha rasmiy ro'yxat yakuniy integratsiya qilinmoqda. Aniq ma'lumot olish uchun operatorimizga ulanishingiz mumkin (/human).
- **Mablag' yechildi, lekin xizmat faollashmadi (Money deducted but service not active):**
  Bu jiddiy moliyaviy masala bo'lgani sababli, biz uni darhol operatorga yo'naltiramiz. Iltimos, /human buyrug'ini bosing yoki biroz kuting.

## Notes
- STRICT SECURITY RULE: Never request or record user payment credentials.
- Financial disputes must always be flagged for human escalation.
