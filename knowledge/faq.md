# General FAQ (Umumiy Ko'p Beriladigan Savollar)

## Official information
- Zynygram is the official social platform combining Uzbek community interaction with state-of-the-art creative AI tools.
- Official support is available via this Telegram bot.
- All inquiries are handled either by Zynygram AI Support or escalated to our human support team.

## Procedures
- Finding answers: Users can ask questions in Uzbek, Russian, or English directly in this chat.
- Connecting to human operators: Send the command `/human` at any time to pause AI responses and request human support.
- Checking conversation status: Send the command `/status` to view the status of your current support session.

- **Zynygram bepulmi? (Is Zynygram free?)**
  Zynygram ijtimoiy tarmog'ida ro'yxatdan o'tish va asosiy funksiyalardan foydalanish bepul. Qo'shimcha premium xizmatlar yoki maxsus AI limitlari bo'yicha: OFFICIAL POLICY REQUIRED.
- **Qanday qilib operator bilan bog'lanish mumkin? (How to reach operator?)**
  Istalgan vaqtda /human buyrug'ini yuboring. Savolingiz avtomatik tarzda qo'llab-quvvatlash guruhiga yuboriladi.
- **Mening ma'lumotlarim xavfsizmi? (Is my data secure?)**
  Ha, Zynygram foydalanuvchilar maxfiyligi va xavfsizligini ta'minlash choralarini ko'radi. Biz hech qachon shaxsiy parollaringiz yoki maxfiy kodlaringizni so'ramaymiz.
- **Rahmat / Thank you / Спасибо:**
  Arzimaydi! Zynygram xizmatlaridan foydalanganingizdan mamnunmiz. Agar yana savollaringiz bo'lsa, bemalol murojaat qiling!
