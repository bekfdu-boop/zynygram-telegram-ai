# Account Management and Security (Akkaunt va Xavfsizlik)

## Official information
- Each Zynygram user profile is linked to their authenticated identity (such as phone number or Telegram account).
- Users are responsible for maintaining the privacy and security of their accounts.
- Zynygram support representatives and automated assistants will NEVER ask for passwords, Telegram login codes, or SMS one-time pins (OTP).

## Procedures
- Account recovery / Password reset:
  - OFFICIAL POLICY REQUIRED: Detailed account recovery mechanisms, email/phone verification links, and identity confirmation steps.
  - If a user claims their account was hacked, compromised, or unauthorized access occurred, escalate immediately to a human operator via `/human`.
- Updating account profile:
  - Users can update their display name, bio, and avatar within the Zynygram application settings.
  - OFFICIAL POLICY REQUIRED: Username change frequency limits and deletion policies.

## Frequently asked questions
- **Akkauntimni qanday tiklasam bo'ladi? (How to recover account?)**
  Akkauntni tiklash bo'yicha rasmiy texnik tartib hozirda ishlab chiqilmoqda. Agar akkauntingizga kira olmayotgan bo'lsangiz yoki xavfsizlik muammosi bo'lsa, /human buyrug'i orqali operatorga murojaat qiling.
- **Parolimni unutdim (Forgot password):**
  Zynygram ilovasi ichidagi "Parolni unutdingizmi" havolasi orqali harakat qilib ko'ring. Agar tizim kiritmasa, operatorimiz yordam beradi.
- **Yordamchi parol so'raydimi? (Does support ask for password?)**
  Yo'q! Zynygram xodimlari va AI yordamchisi hech qachon parolingizni yoki tasdiqlash kodlarini so'ramaydi. Hech kimga bunday ma'lumotlarni bermang.

## Notes
- Never invent account restoration procedures that bypass security protocols.
- Compromised accounts must trigger immediate human escalation.
